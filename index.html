Privacy policy

Fast Score · by Gr4nt · Last updated 4 November 2026

In short

  • Fast Score has no accounts and asks for no personal information (no name, email, phone number, contacts, photos or location).
  • Your favourites, settings, AI Picks history and recent searches are stored only on your phone.
  • There are no ads, no analytics and no tracking. We do not sell or share personal data.

Who we are

Fast Score is a free live-score app made by Gr4nt ("we"). Questions about this policy: gstephen116@gmail.com.

What is stored on your device

To work, the app keeps the following on your phone, in the app's private storage. It never leaves the device and is deleted when you uninstall the app or clear its data:

  • Teams, matches, players and competitions you follow or pin; your settings (theme, sport, language, odds format, notifications).
  • Your recent searches, and the AI Picks made on your phone with their results.
  • Copies of sports data (fixtures, scores, tables, odds) so screens load quickly and work offline.

Where the app gets sports data

To show scores, tables and odds, the app requests public sports data from these services. These requests contain no personal information, only what is needed to fetch the data (for example a match or competition id). Like any website, these services receive your device's IP address and basic technical information as part of the internet connection; their own privacy policies apply.

Notifications

If you allow notifications, alerts for matches you follow are created on your phone: the app (and its live-score service while a followed match is on) checks the score and shows the alert locally. No push token or identifier is sent to us or anyone else. You can turn alerts off in the app's Settings or in your phone's settings at any time.

Permissions

  • Internet / network state: to load sports data.
  • Notifications: to alert you about matches you follow (optional).
  • Foreground service, exact alarms, run at start-up, wake lock: so live alerts start at kick-off and keep running while a followed match is on, even with the app closed. The service shows a visible notification while it runs and stops when the match ends.
  • Vibration: for alert vibration.

The app does not use your location, camera, microphone, contacts, photos or files.

Odds and AI Picks

Bookmaker odds are shown for information only. Fast Score does not take bets, process payments or link your betting accounts. AI Picks are statistical estimates made on your phone, not betting advice. Gambling is for adults (18+) only; please bet responsibly.

Children

Fast Score is intended for users aged 18 and over because it shows betting odds. We do not knowingly collect any personal information from anyone, including children. See our child safety standards.

Your choices and rights

Because we hold no personal data about you, there is nothing for us to access, correct or delete on our side. You can delete everything the app stores at any time with Settings → Clear cached data, by clearing the app's storage in your phone's settings, or by uninstalling the app. For any question or request, contact gstephen116@gmail.com. This applies wherever you live, including under the GDPR, the UK GDPR, Ghana's Data Protection Act 2012 (Act 843) and US state privacy laws.

Security

All data is fetched over encrypted connections (HTTPS). Data stored on your phone is kept in the app's private storage, which other apps cannot read.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new "Last updated" date.
